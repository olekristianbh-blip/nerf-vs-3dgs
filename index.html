NeRF vs 3D Gaussian Splatting

Same scene, two representations: a continuous radiance field queried along rays, versus explicit anisotropic Gaussians drawn as splats.

Guided Step 1 of 5

Representation

A NeRF stores the scene as a continuous neural field. Ask “what is at (x, y, z)?” and the network returns density and colour.

3D Gaussian Splatting stores the scene as a list of anisotropic ellipsoids — explicit primitives you can draw directly.
